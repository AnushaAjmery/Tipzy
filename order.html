How to measure

  1. Cut a thin strip of paper tape or use a soft tape measure.
  2. Measure the widest part of each nail, side to side, in mm.
  3. Do not wrap around the curve; lay it flat across the nail.
  4. Measure all 10 nails, as left and right differ.
  5. If between sizes, choose the smaller one and add a note.

Typical adult range: thumb 14–18 mm, pinky 8–10 mm.
